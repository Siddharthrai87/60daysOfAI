Section 1: What is Prompt Engineering?
Imagine you're asking a very knowledgeable friend for help. If you say "tell me something" — they'll be confused. But if you say "explain compound interest in simple terms with an example" — you get exactly what you need.
Prompt Engineering is simply the skill of asking AI the right way. It's about crafting your instructions clearly so the AI understands exactly what you want, how you want it, and for whom.
You don't need to code. You don't need a tech degree. You just need to learn how to communicate with AI more intentionally.

Section 2: Weak vs Improved Prompt
Weak prompt: "Write about leadership"
Improved prompt: "You are a leadership coach. Write 5 practical tips for first-time managers struggling with team communication. Keep each tip to 2 sentences. Use simple, encouraging language."

Three Practical Benefits of Better Prompts

2. Get higher quality results — Specific prompts produce specific, relevant answers — not generic filler.
