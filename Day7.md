Claude is a family of AI models developed by Anthropic. The models are generally divided into Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus tiers, arranged from fastest and cheapest to most powerful. Anthropic periodically releases newer versions (e.g., Claude 3, 3.5, 3.7, 4.x).<br><br>
Why should we select the appropriate AI model for a given task?
Selecting the appropriate AI model improves efficiency by minimizing response time and token consumption while ensuring that the output has the required accuracy, reasoning capability, and depth for the subject matter.
