## SECTION 12: Interview Talking Points

**Most Impressive Achievements (lead with these)**
1. GATE CSE 2026 — AIR 4817, score 552 (signals strong CS fundamentals, not just web skills)
2. 350+ DSA problems solved across LeetCode and CodeChef, 40+ rated contests, CodeChef rating 1574
3. Coordinated logistics for a 500+ attendee IEEE international conference (CICTN)
4. Two fully built, dependency-free client-side applications with real feature depth

**Key Stories to Discuss**
- **Password Generator:** Explain the decision to use the Web Crypto API instead of `Math.random()` — this signals security awareness, a strong differentiator for an entry-level candidate. Be ready to explain why cryptographically secure randomness matters for password generation.
- **Money Tracker:** Discuss the real-time balance update logic via DOM manipulation without page reloads, and the localStorage persistence decision — frame this as a deliberate architecture choice (client-side state management), not just "I built an app."
- **CICTN Conference:** Your go-to leadership story. Use STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and have one specific cross-team coordination or logistics problem ready to describe in detail.
- **GFG 160-Day Streak:** Frame this as evidence of consistency and self-discipline sustained over time, not just raw problem count.

**Areas Recruiters Are Likely to Ask About**
- *"You don't have a framework like React on your resume — how quickly could you pick one up?"* → Reference your concrete learning plan (Section 10) confidently.
- *"Tell me about a time you had to debug something tricky."* → Have one specific example ready from either project (e.g., an edge case in input validation or balance calculation).
- *"What's your experience with databases?"* → Be upfront that it's coursework-level (DBMS) so far, then pivot to your plan to apply it through a real project.
- *"Why web development, given your DSA/GATE strength?"* → Prepare a genuine, specific answer — your profile shows strength in both directions, and interviewers will probe this.
- *"Walk me through your CICTN role."* → Have 2–3 concrete responsibilities ready beyond "coordinated logistics" (e.g., a specific scheduling conflict resolved, vendor or speaker communication handled).
