Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

7 speakers • CEO, Head of Sales, CFO, VP Marketing, Product Director, CTO, Customer Success Lead • Source: uploaded transcript (pdf.pdf)
Summary
The leadership team reviewed Q2 results, where revenue grew 12%, below the 18% annual target, largely because only 9 of 14 expected enterprise contracts closed due to procurement delays and added security reviews. Marketing reported 34% traffic growth but weaker conversion, prompting a proposed website redesign — though Engineering flagged limited capacity while building the August analytics dashboard. Customer Success reported 94% retention alongside rising support tickets tied to reporting performance and dashboard loading times, which the team flagged as a priority. Hiring decisions and the annual conference budget were both postponed pending further inputs (Q3 forecasts and vendor proposals, respectively), and no public commitment was made on the dashboard's August release date.
