📝 Executive Summary

Analysis of 10 major Indian cities (June 2026) reveals a stark North-South pollution divide. The average AQI across all cities is 117 (Moderate), masking extreme variation: while Mumbai records a healthy AQI of 53 owing to monsoon-season sea breezes, your home city of Dādri records 197 (Poor) — the highest in this dataset — driven by the NTPC Dadri thermal power plant, NCR industrial activity, and stagnant Gangetic Plain air dynamics. PM2.5 levels in Dādri are approximately 9.6× above WHO annual guidelines. South Indian metros (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad) consistently outperform northern cities, with AQIs clustering in the Moderate-to-Satisfactory band. 66 out of 100 of the world's most polluted cities are in India (IQAir 2025 World Report), underscoring the scale of the public health challenge. Immediate personal protective action is strongly recommended for Dādri residents.

💊 Personalized Recommendations for Dādri, UP
🌅
Daily Actions
  • Check AQI on aqi.in or IQAir every morning before stepping out
  • Wear N95/FFP2 mask outdoors — surgical masks are insufficient for PM2.5
  • Limit outdoor exposure to 6–9 AM when AQI is relatively lower
  • Shower after returning indoors to remove particulate residue from skin and hair
  • Stay hydrated — 3+ litres of water daily to aid lung clearance
🏠
Indoor Air Improvements
  • Use HEPA air purifier (CADR ≥ 300 for a 200 sq ft room) — essential, not optional
  • Keep windows shut during peak pollution hours (7–10 AM, 6–9 PM)
  • Add spider plants, peace lilies, or snake plants as natural air filters
  • Avoid burning incense, candles, or cooking on biomass indoors
  • Regularly replace AC filters; use PM2.5-rated cabin filters
🏃
Outdoor Activity Guidance
  • Reschedule runs or workouts to late evening (post-9 PM) or early morning
  • Avoid roads near NTPC Dadri plant, industrial zones, and highways
  • On high-AQI days (>150), switch to indoor workouts entirely
  • Cyclists and runners: always wear N95; exertion increases breathing rate 10×
  • Check GRAP alerts — Stage 3/4 means no outdoor strenuous activity
💆
Hair Care (High Risk Zone)
  • Wash hair every 2 days — PM2.5 deposits on scalp cause inflammation and hair fall
  • Use a sulphate-free, scalp-soothing shampoo with salicylic acid or tea tree
  • Apply argan or coconut oil as a protective barrier before going outdoors
  • Avoid heat styling — pollution already weakens hair shafts considerably
  • Cover hair with a scarf or hat in high-pollution areas; consider a silk lined hat
✨
Skin Care Recommendations
  • Double-cleanse every evening — pollution particles clog pores deeply
  • Use antioxidant serum (Vitamin C) in the morning to neutralize free radicals
  • SPF 50+ sunscreen is mandatory even on cloudy days in polluted cities
  • Niacinamide-based moisturizer strengthens skin barrier against PM damage
  • Avoid touching face outdoors — hands carry particulate from surfaces
💧
Water Quality Improvements
  • Install a multi-stage RO+UV water purifier — tap water in NCR is hard and contaminated
  • Test water annually for TDS, fluoride, arsenic, and heavy metals
  • For hair: use a shower filter (vitamin C type) to reduce chlorine and hardness
  • Rinse hair with mineral or filtered water for last rinse to reduce scalp irritation
  • For drinking: TDS between 150–300 ppm is ideal; above 500 TDS needs RO
